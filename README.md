## Proposed System Features

### 1. Digital Visitor Registration
A web-based visitor registration interface designed for the library's iPad and other supported devices. Visitors will be able to enter their details, select a reason for visiting, and register their arrival electronically, reducing reliance on the paper-based register.

### 2. Visitor Sign-In and Sign-Out Tracking
The system will record visitor sign-in and sign-out times to help staff monitor daily library attendance and identify the number of visitors currently on the premises.

### 3. Automated Statistics and Analytics
The system will calculate visitor totals, unique visitor counts, visit frequency, current occupancy, and visit trends using recorded registration data. Where applicable, statistics will be grouped by day, month, year, and reason for visiting.

### 4. Categorisation of Visit Reasons
Visitors will be able to select a reason for their visit, such as studying, research, reading, using internet or computing facilities, typing, or accessing other library facilities. This information will support analysis of how library resources and services are used.

### 5. Secure Administrator Authentication
Access to administrative functions will be restricted to authorized personnel. Authentication and authorization will be implemented through the backend to protect visitor information and prevent unauthorized access to administrative records.

### 6. Administrator Dashboard
The administrator dashboard will provide an overview of visitor activity through summary cards, charts, and statistical visualisations. Depending on the approved reporting requirements, the dashboard may display daily, monthly, and yearly statistics, visit reasons, historical comparisons, and current visitor numbers.

### 7. PDF Report Generation and Download
Authorized administrators will be able to generate and download PDF reports containing approved visitor statistics and summaries. Reports will follow the library's agreed reporting template and may include reporting periods, visitor totals, visit categories, and other approved indicators.

### 8. Historical Records and Reporting
The system will support access to historical visitor records and statistics for selected reporting periods, allowing administrators to review previous activity, compare trends, and prepare information for management reporting.

### 9. Visitor Data Management
The system will maintain organised visitor and visit records, subject to the library's data-retention policy. Authorized staff may be provided with appropriate functions for reviewing and correcting records, where approved.

### 10. Data Validation and Record Accuracy
The registration process will include validation to ensure required fields are completed and information is entered in the correct format. Appropriate safeguards will be considered to reduce duplicate or incomplete records and improve the reliability of reporting.

### 11. Backend and Database Integration
The Flutter web frontend will communicate with the backend through agreed API endpoints. The backend will be responsible for processing requests, validating and storing data, enforcing access controls, and supplying the information required by the dashboard and reporting functions.

### 12. User-Friendly and Responsive Interface
The system will provide a clear, accessible interface suitable for use by library visitors and staff. The design will support the library's branding, an iPad-friendly registration experience, and responsive layouts for other supported screen sizes.

### 13. Data Protection and Privacy
The system will be designed to protect visitors' personal information through appropriate access controls, secure communication, and responsible handling of stored records. Data collection, retention, and reporting will follow the library's approved privacy and information-management requirements.

### 14. Future Enhancements
Subject to stakeholder approval and project scope, future enhancements may include advanced reporting, spreadsheet exports, improved search and filtering, audit trails for administrative changes, and integration with other approved library systems.

**Note:** The features listed above are proposed requirements and remain subject to confirmation by the library supervisor and relevant stakeholders. Implementation will depend on the agreed project scope, technical feasibility, privacy requirements, and availability of the necessary backend services and data.
